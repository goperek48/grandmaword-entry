Entry for make.fun's **One Word From Grandma** bounty
(`u-0xkianno-one-word-from-grandma-f04bed16`).

`main.py` writes one original word to `/output/entry.txt`:

> robit

Grandma's typo for "robot" — she means the machine, she spells it the
way she hears it, and the misspelling is the whole joke: warm, a little
baffled, unmistakably a grandmother texting her grandchild about the
robot that lives in the phone now. One word, 5 characters.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.txt` (validated: exactly one word, 1–30 characters,
UTF-8).
